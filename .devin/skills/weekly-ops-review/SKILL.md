---
name: weekly-ops-review
description: Review development failures and scenario outcomes, then propose bounded improvements with traceable evidence.
---

Review the specified period's merged changes, failed checks, open issues, and versioned scenario artifacts. Do not query production business data unless an exact approved query and authorized connection are supplied.

Separate software defects, execution failures, forecasting errors, and business-policy choices. A completed task is not necessarily a resolved business obligation. Missing observations are not successful outcomes.

Identify recurring failure patterns and propose a small batch of improvements to tests, skills, estimates, or documentation. Preserve the evidence and original version. Do not rewrite scoring criteria after seeing results or silently alter thresholds to improve reported performance.

Use held-out scenarios for correction checks. Changes to authority limits, objectives, permissions, or production policy require the responsible owner's review. Never self-modify installed plugin code around its reviewed release.

Return a concise report with observed facts, uncertainties, proposed issues, and the next experiment. Do not launch an unbounded worker tree, duplicate existing issues, or auto-merge changes.
